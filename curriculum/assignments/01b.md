## Public interface

ContactBook exposes add(email,name,phone=""), update(email, name=None, phone=None), search(query), delete(email), save(path), and classmethod load(path). Normalize email by strip/casefold, reject duplicates with ValueError and missing updates with KeyError. Search returns fresh record dictionaries with email/name/phone. Delete returns bool. analyze(text,limit=5) returns characters, words, unique_words, top_words as (word,count) pairs, and most_common (or None); use lowercase ASCII words and alphabetical ties.
