## Public interface

reference_cases() returns >=40 dev and >=20 holdout labels with unique id, question, company, year, field, expected, source_id, group, tenant, category, split, label_status. For the bundled numerical fixture, field is revenue/operating_margin/free_cash_flow. Groups may not overlap between dev and holdout. Labels must match source arithmetic. Broader failure/abstention cases and human review are separate evidence.

```python
reference_cases()
```
