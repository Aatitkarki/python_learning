## Public interface

mean, variance(sample=True), standard_deviation(sample=True), covariance, correlation operate on numeric sequences. Sample covariance uses n-1. Reject insufficient or mismatched samples and undefined constant-vector correlation with ValueError. permutation_test(a,b,repeats,seed) tests the absolute mean difference with private seeded randomness and the (extreme+1)/(repeats+1) convention.

```python
mean(xs)
variance(xs, sample=True)
standard_deviation(xs, sample=True)
covariance(xs, ys)
correlation(xs, ys)
permutation_test(a, b, repeats=2000, seed=42)
```
