# Stage 11 — Spark and big data

**Budget:** 40 hours · **Suggested calendar:** weeks 63–65 at 15 hours/week · **Prerequisites:** Pass [Stage 10](10.md) and complete the earlier notebooks in this stage.

## What you will learn

- Driver/executors, lazy plans, transformations/actions, partitions, shuffles, joins, skew, and caching
- PySpark schemas/expressions/windows and avoiding unnecessary Python UDFs
- CSV/JSON/Parquet/Avro, columnar projection, batch ingestion, and distributed validation

## Study sequence and hours

1. **Learn and explain — 8 hours.** Read the notebook lessons and linked primary references. Produce a one-page explanation with one worked example for each topic above. Use CS50P/CS50 SQL and D2L alongside this course where listed in RESOURCES.md.
2. **Practice and debug — 12 hours.** Complete the notebooks below, then change inputs, add edge tests, and reproduce every important result from a fresh process. Keep a mistake log and repair at least one deliberately introduced bug per notebook.
3. **Build independently — 16 hours.** Implement the project brief from an empty folder under work/. Produce the deliverables below and compare with the reference only after a serious attempt.
4. **Assess and revisit — 4 hours.** Complete the closed-book gate, explain decisions aloud, and schedule 7/30-day reviews. If the gate fails, use the remediation path and retry with changed inputs.
