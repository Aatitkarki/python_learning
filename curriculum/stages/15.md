**Budget:** 90 hours · **Suggested calendar:** weeks 75–80 at 15 hours/week · **Prerequisites:** Pass [Stage 14](14.md) and complete the earlier notebooks in this stage.

## What you will learn

- End-to-end architecture, typed orchestration, verified financial tools, provenance, and local generation
- PostgreSQL/pgvector, hybrid search/reranking, identity/permissions, state, Docker, monitoring, and recovery
- 100–500 curated evaluations over time, incident drills, independent implementation, portfolio evidence, and oral defense

## Study sequence and hours

1. **Learn and explain — 18 hours.** Read the notebook lessons and linked primary references. Produce a one-page explanation with one worked example for each topic above. Use CS50P/CS50 SQL and D2L alongside this course where listed in RESOURCES.md.
2. **Practice and debug — 27 hours.** Complete the notebooks below, then change inputs, add edge tests, and reproduce every important result from a fresh process. Keep a mistake log and repair at least one deliberately introduced bug per notebook.
3. **Build independently — 36 hours.** Implement the project brief from an empty folder under work/. Produce the deliverables below and compare with the reference only after a serious attempt.
4. **Assess and revisit — 9 hours.** Complete the closed-book gate, explain decisions aloud, and schedule 7/30-day reviews. If the gate fails, use the remediation path and retry with changed inputs.

## If you get stuck

Return to the earliest failed boundary: Python/data before models, retrieval before generation, deterministic tools before autonomous routing. Retest after 7 and 30 days.

Explain what failed, show the smallest reproduction, and ask for a hint. Do not replace your entire solution with an answer file. Rebuild the corrected idea after 48 hours and include one unseen case.
