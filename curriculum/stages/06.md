# Stage 06 — Transformers and LLMs

**Budget:** 100 hours · **Suggested calendar:** weeks 39–45 at 15 hours/week · **Prerequisites:** Pass [Stage 05](05.md) and complete the earlier notebooks in this stage.

## What you will learn

- Character/subword tokenization, embeddings, positions, Q/K/V, causal/multi-head attention, residuals, and normalization
- Encoder/decoder/encoder-decoder, next-token loss, context, greedy/beam/top-k/top-p/temperature, and KV cache
- Pretraining/SFT, full fine-tuning, LoRA/QLoRA/PEFT, FP32/FP16/BF16/INT8/INT4, and structured outputs

## Study sequence and hours

1. **Learn and explain — 20 hours.** Read the notebook lessons and linked primary references. Produce a one-page explanation with one worked example for each topic above. Use CS50P/CS50 SQL and D2L alongside this course where listed in RESOURCES.md.
2. **Practice and debug — 30 hours.** Complete the notebooks below, then change inputs, add edge tests, and reproduce every important result from a fresh process. Keep a mistake log and repair at least one deliberately introduced bug per notebook.
3. **Build independently — 40 hours.** Implement the project brief from an empty folder under work/. Produce the deliverables below and compare with the reference only after a serious attempt.
4. **Assess and revisit — 10 hours.** Complete the closed-book gate, explain decisions aloud, and schedule 7/30-day reviews. If the gate fails, use the remediation path and retry with changed inputs.
